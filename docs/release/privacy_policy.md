# Privacy Policy for ClapReply

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-23

ClapReply ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ClapReply mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## 1. Information Collection

We do **NOT** collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics.
- **No Account Required:** You can use the App without creating an account.
- **Local Processing:** All "vibe" selection and history storage happens locally on your device.
- **AI Processing:** When you generate a reply, the text of the tweet you provide is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. It is not stored by us.

## 2. Data Storage

- **Local Storage:** Your generated replies and history are stored locally on your device using Hive. If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted.

## 3. Third-Party Services

- **Google Gemini API:** We use Google's Generative AI to generate replies. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information on how they handle data.

## 4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@clapreply.app
