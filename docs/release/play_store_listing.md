Struggling to come up with the perfect reply? **ClapReply** helps you enter the conversation with impact! 🚀

Whether you want to be witty, insightful, playful, or just agree with style, ClapReply generates high-quality, engaging replies in seconds using advanced AI.

**✨ Key Features:**
*   **Multiple Vibes:** Choose from Witty, Insightful, Playful, Agree & Amplify, and more.
*   **Instant AI Generation:** Powered by Google Gemini (`gemma-27b-it`) for smart, context-aware responses.
*   **Privacy First:** No login required. Your history is stored locally on your device.
*   **Clean & Simple:** Paste a tweet, pick a vibe, get replies. No clutter.

**🔒 Privacy & Safety:**
We believe in engaging, not harassing. ClapReply is designed to help you contribute positively to conversations. We do not collect any personal user data.
